## 1. Parts sufficient?
Yes. The combination of **Session Space Anchor**, **Plan Basis**, and **Movement Record** templates provides a physical structure that replaces generic analysis/design phases with a Space-aligned loop.

## 2. Plan Basis right?
Yes. It serves as the mandatory grounding gate, forcing the model to justify every proposed movement against existing **Source Anchors** rather than model defaults.

## 3. Broad-but-bounded default?
Yes. This packaging directly addresses the "user-as-relay" failure by ensuring the work unit is self-contained and focused on **Return-to-Space Value**.

## 4. Strongest watch item?
**Boundary Integrity.** Specifically, preventing Plan Mode from slipping into un-tracked execution or bypassing the disclosure of non-inspected evidence.

## 5. Remain HOLD?
Permanent promotion of this stack to higher maturity levels and autonomous "Writer" capabilities for closeout records.
