## 3. Best Positions For Small Anchors

These 4 should travel in the next Session Space Anchor to prevent session convergence and user relay burden:

1. **PV_PLAN_BASIS_GATE**: Forces space-grounding before accepting a plan.
2. **PV_BROAD_BOUNDED_PACKAGE**: Prevents unnecessary session splitting.
3. **PV_NON_INSPECTED_DISCLOSURE**: Maintains provenance integrity during bounded reads.
4. **PV_RETURN_TO_SPACE_CLOSEOUT**: Ensures reusable memory is captured before exit.
