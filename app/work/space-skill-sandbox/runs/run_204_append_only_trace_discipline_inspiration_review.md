## 2. What the Idea Means

Append-only trace means past records are not casually rewritten.

New understanding is added as a later record, so the path of judgment remains visible.

In simple terms:

```text
Do not silently replace the old record.
Add the correction, closeout, or new judgment after it.
That way future sessions can see what changed and why.
```

## 3. Closest Concepts in Our Space

- run records: preserve each bounded read, verdict, closeout, and stop point
- process-memory: preserves why a judgment happened, not only what succeeded
- current-position entries: summarize the latest safe re-entry state without erasing previous anchors
- closeout notes: record when a round is closed and what remains watch-only
- watch items: preserve unresolved risks without forcing immediate action
- Codex/Gemini handoff records: preserve role boundary and execution/observation evidence
- ops trace before memory loss: keep enough trace before session loss so recovery does not depend on chat memory

No new folders, schemas, ledgers, or protocols are proposed.

## 4. What We Can Learn

- `INSPIRATION_ONLY` - preserve the judgment path instead of overwriting it.
- `INSPIRATION_ONLY` - record why something stopped, not only what succeeded.
- `INSPIRATION_ONLY` - keep correction as later trace, not silent replacement.
- `INSPIRATION_ONLY` - distinguish closeout from promotion.
- `INSPIRATION_ONLY` - make handoff history readable without turning it into a ledger.

## 5. What We Must Not Copy

- do not replace our process-memory with one global `work.log`
- do not force every action into append-only ceremony
- do not treat append-only as immutable law
- do not turn traces into an official ledger
- do not make Codex the owner of memory truth
