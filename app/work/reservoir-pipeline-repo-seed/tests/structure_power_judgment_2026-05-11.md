## Corrected Test Question

The question is not:

```text
Is the space organized?
```

The question is:

```text
What power does the structure we built actually have?
```

Power means the structure can change what future work becomes possible.

## Power Criteria

Judge the structure by whether it can:

```text
1. preserve intent across time
2. let a future worker understand why a pipeline exists
3. pull bounded material from a large space without flattening the space
4. attach a temporary pipeline without turning it into source-space law
5. generate a derivative artifact in sandbox
6. return the result as usable judgment
7. prevent misuse, over-promotion, and false authority
8. make the next pipeline easier to create
```

## Power 2 - Reservoir Preservation

Verdict:

```text
STRONG
```

