## 3. Executive synthesis
The project currently possesses a robust collection of **maturing process assets** and **comparison lenses**, but no established pipelines. Stability is anchored in the **User-as-Judge** decision structure. Growth is occurring through the crystallization of reusable procedural grammar (e.g., Formation Prework, Mistake Recording). The space is currently a "maturation incubator" where execution signals are successfully being converted into structural support, though most elements are held as "Watch-only" to prevent premature hardening into law or automation.
