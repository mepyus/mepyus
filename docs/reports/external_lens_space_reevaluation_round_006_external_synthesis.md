## 3. User Space Re-described from the Outside

### 3.1 One-sentence external description
The User Space is a **Sovereign Cognitive Universe** where human attention is the primary unit of currency, and AI acts as a worker-observer that stratifies information into layers of resolution rather than simply storing data for later recall.

### 3.2 Product-category description
It sits at the intersection of **Sensemaking Infrastructure**, **Knowledge Management**, and **Autonomous Orchestration**, but it fundamentally rejects the "App" classification in favor of "Cosmology."

### 3.3 Philosophy description
A **Participatory Cosmology** where the environment (space) forms its own laws (stratification/provenance) through recursive observation by both the human and the AI worker.

### 3.4 Engineering-adjacent description
An **Event-Sourced Knowledge Fabric** that treats CLI interactions as "high-resolution events" and utilizes human sovereign locks to ensure structural integrity across layers.

### 3.5 What it is NOT
- Not a Second Brain (it is not a passive storage silo).
- Not a Knowledge Graph (it does not prioritize links over layers).
- Not an Agent Orchestration (it does not automate the human).

## 4. External Critique Synthesis

### Strengths
- **Resolution-focus:** Prioritizing space density over flat storage.
- **Provenance-Integrity:** Maintaining the lineage of every "fact" in the space.
- **Human Sovereign Lock:** Ensuring authority remains with the user, shielding the space from agent drift.

### Weaknesses
- **Cognitive Load:** Without high-level aggregation, the current "Manual Lock" process is a high-friction bottleneck.
- **Entry Chaos:** The lack of predefined schemas (Reverse Ontology) makes the "Start" state feel disorganized to external observers.

### Ambiguities
- The distinction between "Agent Work" and "Space Evolution" is still unclear to external onlookers.

### Misreading Risks
- External users (accustomed to Notion/Tana) will try to force "Schema-First" thinking, eventually suffocating the universe's natural development.

### Differentiation
- While external tools build **Databases**, we are building a **Cosmology**.
