## round 1 reading

### A. structure_before_freeform_agenting

This candidate remains strongest as a higher operating principle, not as an axis.

Evidence:

- bridge failure note repeatedly insists that lower material is not enough by itself and that purpose and authority must be added explicitly before packet meaning is treated as valid
- package continuity validation says the current state passes only as a minimal spine and thin workbench, and explicitly blocks jumping to a full product or multi-agent surface
- unresolved hold note keeps possible middle cases open but refuses broad reopening; this is another form of “do not allow freedom before structure”

Why this is not yet an axis:

- it explains ordering and sequencing well
- it does not yet reorganize the space into distinct bounded families on its own
- it acts more like a meta-rule about what must come first

Current layer judgment:

- `higher operating principle`

### B. bounded_surface_over_universal_agent_freedom

This remains the strongest actual axis candidate.

Evidence:

- surface slot closeout explicitly prefers slot-based one-handler stabilization over second-handler expansion or generic dashboard growth
- worker execution log explicitly splits `/operate`, `/team`, `/internal`, and `/synthesis` by surface responsibility and says this is a surface-role separation, not a new runtime flow
- the same log keeps front-level directive control separate from rear observation structures and blocks worker authority promotion

Why this is stronger than a simple rule:

- it has explanation power: it explains why the system repeatedly chooses surfaces, roles, rear/front splits, and bounded operating desks
- it has re-layout power: it can regroup slot notes, worker surface notes, team-role notes, and runtime attach boundaries into one family
- it also explains why “universal freedom” or one mixed dashboard keeps being resisted

Current layer judgment:

- `strongest provisional axis candidate`

### C. maintenance_and_validation_discipline

This still reads more like maturation / verification governance than like an axis.

Evidence:

- unresolved hold note is explicit that unresolved hold is not reopen permission; it keeps pressure visible without letting it widen the whole system
- package continuity validation passes with note, preserves thinness, and names the exact areas that are still too weak instead of overstating completion
- bridge failure note turns repeated failure into reusable warnings while refusing to claim a global taxonomy

Why this is not the same kind of thing as B:

- it does not primarily organize the space into role/surface families
- it primarily controls how things are allowed to mature, pause, hold, or remain bounded
- it shows up as restraint, validation, note-writing, reuse warnings, and overclaim prevention

Current layer judgment:

- `maturation / validation governance grammar`
