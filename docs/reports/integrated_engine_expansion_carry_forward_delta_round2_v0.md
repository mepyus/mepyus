## 5. why no promotion happened

No future axis was promoted because round 2 had a narrow purpose:

- improve styling consistency
- reduce responsive central-gravity risk
- quiet support layers
- keep all panel read mappings unchanged

Promotion would require a separate decision under `integrated_engine_promotion_gate_criteria_v0.md`.

## 6. carry-forward rule

Future expansion may be discussed from this delta, but implementation must first pass the relevant promotion gate and prove that it does not weaken request / return / reflux separation or central panel gravity.
