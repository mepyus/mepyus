## 1. Why This Addendum Exists
Over Rounds 002-016, we analyzed the User Space through various "External Lenses" (Memory, Multi-Agent Friction, Oversight, Ergonomics, Evidence). These findings, while insightful, are scattered across multiple reports. This addendum synthesizes these insights into a stable, persistent inventory of **Thought Assets** (what to build upon) and **Dangerous Assumptions** (what to avoid). It serves as a guide for any future internal design, ensuring we do not regress into "schema-first" or "automation-bias" traps.

## 2. External Identity of User Space
- **Sovereign Cognitive Universe:** A space prioritizing "Space Resolution" (the depth of understanding) over simple data recall.
- **Event-Sourced Knowledge Fabric:** Treats all CLI/Assistant activity as high-resolution events linked to their provenance.
- **Participatory Cosmology:** The space forms its own laws (stratification/provenance) through recursive observation, rather than being an "App" with static features.
- **Layer-Aware Lattice:** A structure where information is stratified by resolution and context, not flattened by tags or schemas.

## 3. Repeated Finding Clusters
1. **Claim vs. Evidence:** Summary without evidence drill-down is an unverified claim. Provenance anchors must survive distillation.
2. **Human Attention:** Attention is the currency of the universe; "Review Fatigue" is the primary enemy of structural integrity.
3. **Ambient Oversight:** Normal states must be backgrounded; only "Off-Nominal" (anomaly) signals surface.
4. **Maturation Vocabulary:** Status signals (e.g., `Provisional`, `Maturing`, `Canonical`) indicate "growth stage," not fixed ontology.
5. **Worker Noise:** Interaction cascades (consequential alarms) must be grouped into a single "Primary Event" to prevent tray fatigue.
6. **Schema-First Risk:** Prematurely locking schemas suffocates the natural maturation of the cosmology.

## 6. Strategic Cautions for Future Design
1. **Human Lock Remains Human:** AI proposes; Human locks. No auto-promotion to baseline.
2. **Summary-Evidence Coupling:** A summary without a direct provenance anchor is a "Claim," not an "Asset."
3. **Secondary Residue Collapse:** Consequential signals are collapsed, not deleted; provenance must remain accessible.
4. **Observer Signal, Not Ontology:** Status vocabulary is a signal to guide attention, not a rigid schema.
5. **Maturation is not Binary:** Ambiguity is an *intentional design resource* during the high-entropy phase.
6. **Provenance Persistence:** Summaries must survive the distillation process without losing the link to raw evidence.
