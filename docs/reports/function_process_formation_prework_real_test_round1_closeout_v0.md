## 2. What Round 1 confirmed
*   **v1 Structural Integrity:** `Formation Prework v1` successfully role-formed two complex external candidates (MCP and AWS) without drifting into adoption or implementation.
*   **Five-Part Unit Utility:** The structure (Candidate Card, Prior Retrieval, Process Asset, Reuse Hook, User Gate) remained usable and effective across different candidate types (Protocol vs. Repository).
*   **Retrieval Boundary Necessity:** `retrieval_scope_boundary` was proven to be a critical aid in preventing broad source expansion and model wandering.
*   **Safe Orientation:** External candidates can be matured as "candidate references" and "operation references," providing immediate value to the space without modifying its baseline or official workflows.

## 3. What Round 1 did not confirm
*   **No Technical Adoption:** Round 1 did not approve the implementation of MCP servers, AWS Strands SDKs, or any technical integration.
*   **No Workflow Promotion:** The candidate signals (Plan Packet, Resource/Tool) were not promoted to baseline or official workflow status.
*   **No Automatic Prework:** The round did not prove that every external material requires a full Prework unit; its application remains a judgmental decision.
*   **No Rigid Ontology:** There is no proof that project assets must be forced into the Resource/Tool or Plan/Execution taxonomies.
