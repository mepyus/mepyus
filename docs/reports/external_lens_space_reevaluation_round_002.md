## 1. Why This Round
The previous research cycle prioritized internal reingestion design (gates/contracts) too early. This round resets to an **External Lens perspective**, ensuring we fully understand how the external world perceives AI, memory, and cognitive space before we finalize any internal structures. The objective is to validate our cosmology against the external landscape to identify "Borrowable Thought Assets" and avoid "Dangerous Product Assumptions."

## 2. User Thought Structure Summary
The user space is a **Self-Forming Universe** based on:
- **Recursive Observation:** CLI outputs feedback into Stage 0 as events.
- **Stratified Resolution:** Increasing the density of meaning through layers.
- **Human Sovereignty:** AI as worker/proposer; Human as lock/sovereign.
- **Provenance-First:** Lineage over flat storage.
- **Reverse Ontology:** Natural emergence over predefined schema.

## 3. External Case Harvest

### A. AI Memory / Personal Context
- **Cases:** Pieces, Mem, Rewind/Limitless, Reflect, mymind.
- **Their View:** AI as an "OS-level Hippocampus" or "Personal Curator."
- **Hidden Assumption:** "Recall = Understanding." The more you store, the smarter the space.
- **Borrowable Asset:** **"Workstream Activity"** (chronological trace) is a high-utility asset if lowered to the `interaction_trace` layer.
- **Dangerous Assumption:** Automatic full-capture leading to "Total Recall Noise."

### B. Cognitive OS
- **Cases:** Notion AI, Capacities, Anytype, Heptabase.
- **Their View:** AI as the "Engine" within an Object-Oriented Workspace.
- **Hidden Assumption:** "Schema-First." Reality must be typed/tagged before it is useful.
- **Borrowable Asset:** **"Object-based Contextual Links."**
- **Dangerous Assumption:** "Schema Suffocation" where reality is forced into predefined categories (reverse ontology is safer).

### C. Thinking Environment / Sensemaking
- **Cases:** Roam, Obsidian, Zettelkasten, Progressive Summarization.
- **Their View:** Tools to expand the user's "Cognitive Reach."
- **Hidden Assumption:** "Explicit Linking" is the only path to intelligence.
- **Borrowable Asset:** **"Atomic Notes"** (single-purpose nodes for high reusability).
- **Dangerous Assumption:** "Graph-based Obsession" (where the link matters more than the layer).

### D. Human Augmentation / Philosophy
- **Cases:** Engelbart, Dynamicland, Memex.
- **Their View:** AI/Computer as a *Place* or *Medium* for collective thought.
- **Hidden Assumption:** The environment itself is the interface.
- **Borrowable Asset:** **"Computer as a Place"** (The strongest conceptual alignment).
- **Dangerous Assumption:** "Physicality Obsession" (hard to replicate in CLI-text environments).

### E. Agentic Workspace
- **Cases:** LangGraph, Letta, CrewAI, MCP.
- **Their View:** AI as an autonomous actor with its own RAM/Disk.
- **Hidden Assumption:** "Agentic Autonomy." The AI should manage its own state.
- **Borrowable Asset:** **"Stateful Checkpoints."**
- **Dangerous Assumption:** "Self-Editing Memory" (Letta style) without Human Lock is an existential threat to space sovereignty.

## 6. External Critique of User Space

### Strengths Seen from Outside
- **Resolution-focus:** Most external tools are "Recovery-focused." The User Space's focus on "Space Resolution" is a rare, high-signal differentiator.
- **Layer-Awareness:** The ability to differentiate between "Philosophy," "Operation," and "Implementation" is significantly more robust than flat, object-based external schemas.

### Weaknesses Seen from Outside
- **Review Friction:** Without an "Aggregation Threshold," the "Human Sovereign Observer" faces inevitable fatigue.
- **Entry Barrier:** The lack of a "Fixed Structure" can feel chaotic to new users accustomed to Notion/Tana.

### Misunderstanding Risk
- External users might mistake our "Self-Forming Universe" for "Unstructured Data Junk." We must communicate that *structure* is the *goal*, not the starting point.

### Differentiation
- While external tools build *databases*, we are building a *cosmology*.

### Borrowable Assets
- **Progressive Summarization** (as a technique for our `residue` layering).
- **Fact Rating** (Zep-style) as a soft signal for human review.
- **Temporal Checkpoints** (LangGraph-style) for state stratification.

### Do-not-borrow Assumptions
- **"Agent Autonomy"** (Letta-style).
- **"Schema-First"** (Tana/Anytype).
- **"Auto-ingestion"** (Rewind/Limitless).
