## 2. what this round locked

This round locked a provisional bridge format:

- internal term
- internal role or meaning
- why it matters in the integrated engine
- what must be preserved
- flattening risk
- boundary reminder
- provisional human bridge note
- do-not-reduce-to
- scenario scope
- confidence level
- evidence sources

The important lock is not the wording of the bridge notes.

The important lock is the preservation discipline:

- do not explain before identifying route / authority / state / boundary
- do not replace internal terms
- do not turn bridge notes into UI copy
- do not let smoother phrasing open build mode
