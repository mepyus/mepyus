# Space Manual Front Validation Closeout v0

## Verdict

`PASS_WITH_NOTE`

## What Was Validated

Two bounded validations were run against the new manual front:

1. internal structure-read
2. external adaptation

## Validation Summary

### 1. Internal Structure-Read

Result:

`PASS`

Meaning:

- the front manuals were enough to retrieve source intent and runtime evidence
- the system could produce a bounded reading of current direction without dropping into deep reference layers

### 2. External Adaptation

Result:

`PASS_WITH_NOTE`

Meaning:

- the front manuals were enough to choose the right retrieval path and reuse stance
- but the actual adaptation answer still needed package-level reports produced during execution

## What The Manual Front Is Proven To Do

The front is now proven for:

- product understanding
- job-based entry
- role-based asset retrieval
- bounded attach / reuse framing
- output / reinjection framing

## What It Is Not Proven To Replace

The front does not replace:

- package-specific reports
- detailed unresolved boundary notes
- implementation feasibility analysis
- runtime integration testing

That is correct and should remain true.

## Current Judgment

The manual front is strong enough to serve as the first operating layer for:

- internal structure reads
- external-to-space adaptation starts

It is not the final layer for:

- unresolved adjudication
- full attach experiments
- deep boundary work

## Next Safe Use

Use the front first.

Then:

- stop if it already solves the job
- descend into package-specific reports only when the job actually requires them

## Final Closeout

The manual front now works as a real entry surface rather than as a descriptive documentation set.
