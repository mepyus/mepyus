## 5. Reusable Settings vs Formation Prework bridge

The conceptual bridge between these two assets is currently the most critical area for alignment.

### 5.1 Proposed Distinction
- **Reusable Settings:** Shapes that have already become stable through repeated success. They are "off-the-shelf" patterns (e.g., Current Position Entry) that can be copied and adjusted.
- **Function/Process Formation Prework:** The "incubation" process used when a new candidate (material/tool/process) appears. It is used to *form* the candidate's role before it can earn "Reusable Setting" status.

### 5.2 Relationship
- **Overlap:** Both focus on procedural consistency and reducing setup friction.
- **Separation:** Formation Prework is a **gate/evaluator**; Reusable Settings are **outcomes/tools**.
- **Maturity Path:** A successful output from a Formation Prework round (if used repeatedly) becomes a candidate for the **Reusable Operating Settings Catalog.**

### 5.3 Requirements for Promotion
- Evidence of successful reuse in at least 3 distinct scenarios.
- Explicit User-gated approval to move from "Candidate Prework" to "Reusable Setting."
