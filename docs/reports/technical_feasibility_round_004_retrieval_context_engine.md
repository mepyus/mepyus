# Technical Feasibility Round 004
# Topic: Retrieval, Context Engine, and Memory Selection

## 0. Research Declaration
- **Mode:** Web research first, external view first.

## 1. Why Round 004
Retrieval is the mechanism that keeps the space "awake." If we retrieve too little, we are blind; too much, and we suffer from "Total Recall Noise."

## 2. Findings
- **Contextual Retrieval:** Retrieval based on the *active layer* (e.g., operational vs. philosophical).
- **Reranking:** AI-driven selection of the "Top N" most relevant pieces of evidence for the human lock.
- **Memory Compression:** Using summarization to reduce volume without losing context.

## 3. Risks
- "Prompt stuffing" where the AI is overwhelmed with context.
