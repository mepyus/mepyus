## 5. Asset-by-Asset Analysis

### Asset: Human Sovereign Lock
- **Why it matters:** Protects the space from agent drift.
- **External method that supports it:** **Confidence-gated Review (Zep/Active Learning).** It filters the "Sovereign Tray" so humans only review high-risk/low-confidence items.
- **External method that threatens it:** **Letta/Self-editing memory.** Autonomous editing of its own memory is a sovereignty violation.
- **Judgment:** Strong thought asset, requires caution on auto-suppression.

### Asset: Provenance-first
- **Why it matters:** Claims without evidence are unverified.
- **External method that supports it:** **Event Sourcing / Git history.** Perfect audit trails.
- **External method that threatens it:** **Total Recall/Summarization.** Losing lineage during summarization.
- **Judgment:** Core fit for our "Event-Sourced Knowledge Fabric."

### Asset: Layer/Lens-first
- **Why it matters:** Avoiding premature schema lock.
- **External method that supports it:** **Gradual Formalization.** Structure follows pattern emergence.
- **External method that threatens it:** **Knowledge Graph/Semantic Layer.** Can force premature ontology locking.
- **Judgment:** Comparison asset; use as a "reading lens," not a storage structure.

## 6. Dangerous Assumption Inventory
- **"Automation reduces friction":** Automation shifts friction to "verifying the automation."
- **"Confidence equals truth":** AI confidence is uncertainty, not accuracy.
- **"Total capture is memory":** Storing everything creates a noisy archive, not a high-resolution universe.
- **"Sovereignty equals manual bottleneck":** Human authority is an integrity protocol, not a process hurdle.
- **"Schema is Reality":** Premature locking stifles the natural growth of the cosmology.
