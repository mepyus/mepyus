## 3. Executive synthesis
When read through the four axes, the current space reveals itself as a **Sovereign Maturation Incubator**. It is a space where stability is found in the **User-as-Judge** authority structure, while growth is driven by **Procedural Grammar (Process Assets)** rather than functional code. It is currently at a high-resolution alignment point, holding several "Pipeline-Ready" candidates in a non-authoritative state until natural project triggers justify their activation.

## 5. Axis-by-axis deep reading

### Axis 1: Cognitive Harness & Containment
- **what is working:** The "Continue-Until-Blocked" logic and worker evidence packaging. Raw tool output is effectively sandboxed before synthesis.
- **what is maturing:** Explicit stop conditions (Authority Risk, Promotion Risk) are becoming standardized in packets.
- **what is thin:** System-wide registry of harnesses; currently, harnesses are task-specific.
- **what is at risk of over-hardening:** The "13-field packet" becoming a mandatory ceremony for even the simplest tasks.
- **what should remain watch-only:** Automated output filtering/compression logic.
- **natural trigger:** Worker output flooding or overrun in a future execution.

### Axis 2: Sovereign Decoupling
- **what is working:** The separation of "Brain" (Gemini) from "Hand" (CLI) and "Memory" (Space).
- **what is maturing:** The use of "Resource vs Tool" (MCP lens) to clarify execution boundaries.
- **what is thin:** Clear performance thresholds for when to elevate worker roles.
- **what is at risk of over-hardening:** The "User-as-Judge" principle being used to justify lack of technical optimization.
- **what should remain watch-only:** Managed Agent architecture or provider-specific routing.
- **natural trigger:** A decision involving new tool attachment or provider switching.

### Axis 3: Layered Maturation & Reuse
- **what is working:** The 5-part Formation Prework unit as an incubator for external candidates.
- **what is maturing:** The "Maturity Path" from Candidate Signal to Reusable Setting.
- **what is thin:** Provenance links for older `docs/reports` (maturation archive).
- **what is at risk of over-hardening:** Every useful habit being forced into a "Process Asset" template.
- **what should remain watch-only:** Automatic promotion or baseline update logic.
- **natural trigger:** Discovery of a "Missing Link" or a repeated task-success signal.

### Axis 4: Structural Affordance & Communication
- **what is working:** Using re-entry summaries and 4-line cards to orient new sessions.
- **what is maturing:** "Description as Affordance" (Evan Moon lens) for worker behavior guidance.
- **what is thin:** Standardized vocabulary for "Phase-based HITL" (AWS lens).
- **what is at risk of over-hardening:** Turning comparison lenses (Resource/Tool) into a rigid project ontology.
- **what should remain watch-only:** Plan-before-Execution as a mandatory workflow step.
- **natural trigger:** New external candidate intake or complex multi-step coordination.

## 6. Cross-axis tensions
- **Harness vs Affordance:** A tight harness (containment) may reduce the affordance (action-invitation) seen by the worker, leading to "Safety-driven paralysis."
- **Maturation vs Speed:** The requirement for Formation Prework before attachment creates friction for quick, useful tool adoption.
- **Decoupling vs Context:** Too much brain-hand decoupling can lead to workers lacking the necessary context to perform high-resolution observations.
