# Reusable Settings and Formation Prework Bridge v0

## 1. Purpose
This bridge note clarifies the relationship between **Reusable Settings** and **Function/Process Formation Prework** to prevent conceptual overlap and future structural drift. By defining the boundary between "forming a new capability" and "reusing a stable operating shape," we ensure that useful signals are not prematurely hardened into laws, and that established tools remain accessible without redundant evaluation.

Core goals:
*   Avoiding conceptual overlap between evaluation processes and stable outcomes.
*   Preventing premature promotion of immature candidates to "setting" status.
*   Keeping useful operating shapes available for copy-and-adjust reuse.
*   Ensuring immature candidates remain contained within the Prework gate.
*   Preserving the User as the final judge for promotion and adoption.

## 2. Definitions

### 2.1 Formation Prework
Formation Prework is the **gate and incubator** used when a new material, tool, function, or process arrives. It is used to *form* the candidate's role inside the space before any attachment or implementation occurs.
*   **Candidate Intake:** Identify what arrived and why.
*   **Prior Record Retrieval:** Contextualize using `retrieval_scope_boundary`.
*   **Process Asset Unit:** Record the evaluation process itself as a reusable asset.
*   **Reuse Hook:** Define when this specific evaluation should be reread.
*   **User Decision Gate:** Return the final choice to the User.

### 2.2 Reusable Settings
Reusable Settings are **stable, off-the-shelf operating shapes** that have already shown repeated usefulness. They are procedural patterns that can be copied and adjusted for new tasks.
*   **Already Repeated:** Proved useful in multiple distinct scenarios.
*   **Copyable Shape:** Safe to reuse as a template for action or recording.
*   **Support Pattern:** Acts as a friction-reduction tool, not system law.
*   **Not a Registry:** Descriptive of "how we worked," not a mandatory "how you must work."

## 3. Relationship
The relationship is a cycle of maturation and support:
*   **Maturation:** Formation Prework produces process assets and candidate signals that may later mature into **Reusable Settings**.
*   **Support:** Reusable Settings provide the "tools" (e.g., Bounded Reread shape) used during **Formation Prework** to evaluate new candidates.

Key distinctions:
*   Not every Prework output becomes a Reusable Setting (some are one-time evaluations).
*   Not every Reusable Setting needs a new Prework loop (once stable, they are simply copied).

## 4. Maturity Path
This interpretive path describes how a signal moves from discovery to stable reuse without bypassing the User gate:

1.  **Candidate Discovery:** Raw material, tool, or function candidate appears.
2.  **Formation Prework:** Bounded evaluation of role, risk, and fit.
3.  **Process Asset / Candidate Signal:** The result is recorded as useful memory.
4.  **Repeated Safe Use:** The shape is used successfully in 2-3 additional cases.
5.  **Reusable Setting Candidate:** Proposed for the Reusable Operating Settings Catalog.
6.  **User-Gated Reuse:** User approves the shape as a stable "Setting."

## 5. Promotion Caution
### 5.1 Evidence Supporting Promotion to Reusable Setting
*   Successful reuse across at least 3 distinct scenarios.
*   Clear reduction in setup friction or review effort.
*   Stable field definitions (no significant changes needed during reuse).
*   Low risk of authority drift or automation bias.
*   User-recognized usefulness.

### 5.2 Factors Blocking Promotion
*   One-off success or recent "excitement" without repeated proof.
*   Unclear boundaries or high risk of the shape being misread as law.
*   Dependency on a single specific case or worker.
*   Requirement for implementation or current-position update to function.
*   User has not explicitly selected the shape for the catalog.

## 6. Bidirectional Use

### 6.1 Prework → Settings
When a specific evaluation process (e.g., "Space Intake Worklist") shows it can handle diverse materials without failing, its structure is harvested and moved into the Reusable Settings Catalog.

### 6.2 Settings → Prework
When evaluating a new candidate, we use Reusable Settings to bound the task:
*   **Four-Line Card:** Shapes the user-facing output of the prework.
*   **Retrieval Scope Boundary:** Prevents the prework from becoming a broad crawl.
*   **Mistake Recording:** Captures drift during the evaluation process.

## 7. Boundary Map

| Item | Can do | Must not become |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Formation Prework** | Evaluate role and fit of candidates. | Mandatory system workflow. |
| **Reusable Setting** | Provide a copyable operating shape. | Baseline authority or system law. |
| **Reuse Hook** | Assist in retrieving prior records. | Autonomous task router. |
| **Process Asset** | Preserve the "how" of a judgment. | Official ledger or truth source. |
| **Candidate Card** | Aid in metadata-level evaluation. | Formal registry entry. |
| **User Gate** | Provide final decision surface. | Ceremonial or hidden checkbox. |

## 8. User-facing Explanation
*   **Prework**는 씨앗을 심기 전에 토양과 위치를 보는 **'사전 형성 과정'**입니다. (입구)
*   **Reusable Setting**은 이미 몇 번 성공적으로 자라본 방식이라 다시 쓸 수 있는 **'재배법(운영 모양)'**에 가깝습니다. (숙성된 출력)
*   하지만 재배법도 법은 아니며, 특정 상황에 맞춰 변형해서 사용하는 **'판단 보조 도구'**입니다.

## 9. Current Examples

*   **Function/Process Formation Prework v1:** The primary **formation gate** for all new candidates.
*   **Four-Line Card:** A **reusable setting candidate** (High maturity, repeated success).
*   **Mistake Recording:** A **maturing process asset** (Moving toward reusable setting status).
*   **Continue-Until-Blocked:** A **reusable candidate** (Useful, but with high watch-risk for workflow drift).
*   **Whole-space bounded reread:** A **useful shape** (Stable in this round, not yet a setting).

## 10. Watch Items
*   Reusable settings being misread as **baseline architecture**.
*   Formation prework becoming a **mandatory, heavy workflow** for simple tasks.
*   Maturity paths being treated as **automatic promotion systems**.
*   Reuse hooks drifting into **autonomous routing logic**.
*   Process assets being over-read as **official registries or ledgers**.
*   The User Gate becoming a **meaningless ceremony** through agent confidence.

## 11. Do Not Do Yet
*   no automation / runtime scripts
*   no registry / index / ledger
*   no formal schema / official workflow
*   no current-position update / baseline promotion
*   no tool / API / function attachment
*   no package movement / Run 117 approval
*   no broad whole-space crawl
