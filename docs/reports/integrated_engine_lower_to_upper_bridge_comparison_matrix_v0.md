# Integrated Engine Lower To Upper Bridge Comparison Matrix v0

## 1. Verdict

PASS_WITH_NOTE

Two bridge examples now exist. Both worked in a bounded sense, and both remain dependency-heavy. The repeated pattern is stable enough to support supervisory discipline, but not mature enough for automation, unification, or canonical bridge declaration.

## 3. What The Comparison Proves

- Lower bundles can carry real evidence upward.
- Bundle family changes what survives directly.
- Upper purpose, authority, action, output, and route remain structurally upper-added.
- Different lower bundles trigger different overread risks.
- Both examples support a bounded bridge discipline.

## 4. What The Comparison Does Not Prove

- It does not prove a canonical bridge.
- It does not prove runtime automation.
- It does not prove upper/lower unification readiness.
- It does not prove every lower bundle is bridge-eligible.
- It does not prove lower artifacts are already packets.

## 5. Phase 3 Validation

- Real comparison check: passed. The two examples differ by bundle family and risk profile.
- Selective evidence check: passed. The repeated dependency-heavy result is preserved.
- No overgeneralization check: passed. The note supports bridge discipline, not bridge completion.
