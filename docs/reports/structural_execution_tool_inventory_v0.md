## 3. Executive summary
The space utilizes a tiered system of structural tools to manage material intake, interpretation, maturation, and handoff. We are currently utilizing "operating shapes" rather than a formal automated pipeline. The most stable tools are those related to `Formation Prework` (input gating) and `Evidence Packaging` (output containment), while "Pipeline Candidates" are still experimental shapes. The system effectively separates action from context, allowing the project to evolve its logic without hardening it into rigid system law.

## 5. Category summaries

*   **Intake / Ingress:** `Formation Prework` is the strongest intake tool. It creates a necessary friction gate that prevents material from simply "landing" in the space without purpose.
*   **Reading / Interpretation:** `Four-Axis Maps` and `Bounded Deep Rereads` are highly mature. They are useful for establishing orientation but risk becoming "governing laws" if they are updated too frequently.
*   **Process Asset / Maturation:** `Mistake-Memory` is the most effective asset for long-term stability. It turns errors into data.
*   **Pipeline Candidate:** `Continue-Until-Blocked` is the most tested pipeline structure, but requires very strict User gates to prevent it from becoming an autonomous router.
*   **Handoff / Return:** `Worker Evidence Packaging` is the most critical Handoff tool. It ensures the User is the ultimate authority receiver.
*   **Authority / Boundary:** `User-as-Judge` gate is the only absolute authority anchor.
*   **Reference / Comparison:** `Resource/Tool` and `Plan-before-Execution` are stable comparison lenses.
