## 2. What kind of object this transcript is

`saltlux_ai.txt` is not a page shell like `officeout.jsx`.
It is a **conceptual transition hub transcript**.

It organizes:

- AI paradigm history
- scaling arguments
- economic reasoning
- deployment constraints
- document AI as bridge material
- public / enterprise / sovereign AI conditions

So its ontology is not job-state transition.
It is paradigm transition.

## 3. The dominant spine

The transcript advances through a clear conceptual spine:

1. legacy AI paradigms are recalled
2. logical AI / ontology / expert systems are described as an early failed wave
3. semantic web is positioned as an intermediate vision
4. transformer and large language models are presented as the generative wave
5. agentic AI is framed as the next operational wave
6. physical AI is added as the next deployment horizon
7. the cost curve and scaling law are used to explain why the field changed
8. document AI is introduced as the operational bridge that makes AI-ready data possible
9. public / enterprise / security / on-prem constraints are positioned as the adoption boundary

This is a transition ontology of AI eras and adoption conditions.

## 4. Ontological reading by layer

### 4.1 Paradigm history layer

The transcript moves from:

- logical AI
- semantic web
- generative AI
- agentic AI
- physical AI

This is a historical ordering of paradigm transitions, not just a list of technologies.

### 4.2 Scaling / capability layer

The transcript repeatedly uses scaling logic:

- model size
- architecture scaling
- pretraining scale
- post-training
- test-time scaling
- inference / reasoning economics

Ontologically, this is the "what changed the field?" layer.

### 4.3 Economic / operational layer

The transcript does not treat AI as pure theory.
It treats AI as something that must be:

- economical
- deployable
- secure
- on-prem compatible
- government / enterprise viable

This is the adoption boundary layer.

### 4.4 Document AI bridge layer

The transcript's most practical hinge is document AI.

The role of document AI is to transform non-text or semi-structured document material into machine-readable data.

Ontologically, this is the bridge from raw public/enterprise material to AI-ready operational material.

### 4.5 Agentic transition layer

The transcript argues that modern AI is no longer just "answer generation."
It is becoming:

- reasoning
- planning
- multimodal coordination
- tool use
- goal-directed execution

That is the transcript's action ontology.

### 4.6 Safety / deployment layer

The transcript repeatedly binds AI capability to deployment constraints:

- public sector
- security
- sovereignty
- on-prem
- enterprise strategy

This is not side commentary.
It is part of the claim structure.
