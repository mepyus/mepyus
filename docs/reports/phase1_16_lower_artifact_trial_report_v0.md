## Interpretation

### Where Role Tagging Worked Best

- review/report material with repeated headings and explicit operating claims;
- preprocess comparison components that carry bounded gate/checkpoint reasons.

### Where Line Seed Worked Best

- review/report bundles with adjacent transition + claim or background + objection patterns;
- preprocess comparison artifacts where the correction pressure is explicit.

### Where It Stayed Thin

- short directives and compact memo-like material;
- heading-only units;
- any chunk where lexical role cues are weak.
