## 3. External Patterns Re-evaluated

### 3.1 Supervisor Pattern (Logic vs. Control)
- **External View:** Agents have a "Supervisor" to orchestrate steps.
- **User Space Reframe:** The "Supervisor" is not the AI agent itself, but the **Constraint System** provided by the Space. The Space governs; the AI only proposes.
- **Risk:** Agentic Autonomy (AI deciding its own workflow without Human Lock).

### 3.2 Tail-based Sampling (Outcome-Aware)
- **External View:** Only sample high-value logs (anomalies) to reduce SRE fatigue.
- **User Space Reframe:** Only reingest session outcomes that increase "Space Resolution." Discard mechanical noise.

### 3.3 Progressive Summarization (Knowledge Maturation)
- **External View:** Knowledge refines from fleeting to permanent through layers.
- **User Space Reframe:** Our residues mature from `Raw Trace` -> `Event Candidate` -> `Pattern Candidate` -> `Locked Rule`.

## 4. Addressing "Consequential Alarm Suppression"
Our approach to "Alarm Cascade" is a specific form of **Incident Correlation**:
- **Primary Event:** The high-level intent (e.g., `LabelUpdate`).
- **Consequential Residue:** The 50+ lines of mechanical CLI noise.
- **Suppression Logic:** Collapse secondary residues behind the primary `Evidence Anchor`. Never delete them, but move them out of the "Sovereign Attention Zone."

## 5. Dangerous Assumptions (Updated)
- **"Agent Autonomy is Truth":** Allowing AI to "self-lock" its own baseline proposals. (Correction: Always Human-lock).
- **"Schema = Stability":** Imposing static schemas early on (Ontology Lock-in) is a form of design debt.
- **"Supervisory Agent = Supervisor":** The agent acts as a *candidate proposer*; the user remains the *Sovereign Supervisor*.

## 6. Toward a "Frictionless Interaction Contract" v0.1

3. **Visibility Tiers:** Normal states are ambient; Anomalies are interruptive.
4. **Actionable Triage:** Every record classifies its own "Review Burden" (Validation vs. Sovereign Lock).
