# Content Role Examples v0

## Definition

Typical lower chunk:

- a section that defines the compared path, input kind, or operating term.

Likely role:

- `definition`

## Background

Typical lower chunk:

- source identity preservation, profile explanation, route background.

Likely role:

- `background`

## Main Claim

Typical lower chunk:

- a sentence or block that says the current lower bridge is thin because the middle layer is missing.

Likely role:

- `main_claim`

## Transition / Connective

Typical lower chunk:

- “therefore”, “next stage”, “proceed to”, or adjacent bridge explanation.

Likely role:

- `transition` or `connective`

## Correction / Objection

Typical lower chunk:

- a transcript or report block that corrects a misunderstanding or pushes back against a previous reading.

Likely role:

- `correction` or `objection`

## Axis Support Candidate

Typical lower chunk:

- a block that repeatedly pulls toward one direction but still lacks enough independent support to promote.

Likely role:

- `axis_support_candidate`

Use:

- keep it as support/hold note material, not a promoted axis.
