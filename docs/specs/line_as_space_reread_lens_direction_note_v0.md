# line as space reread lens direction note v0

## verdict

- line is no longer sufficient as a document-fragment reading lens only
- the next directional expansion is to treat line as a `space reread lens`
- this means line should be able to reread repeated meanings across the internal corpus, not just react to single segments

## why this shift matters

So far, line has mainly been used as:

- a reading lens over linked segments
- a way to keep variation visible without collapsing a document into one verdict
- a bounded observation surface for runtime and supervisor/operator readout

That was necessary, but it is no longer enough.

If line stays only at the segment/document level, then:

- the space keeps reacting in its own internal language
- the user must still manually reread the wider corpus to understand what a concept really means in the space
- repeated meanings remain dispersed across notes, reports, transcripts, and declarations

## what changed

Recent rereads of `ontology` and `harness` showed a new requirement.

The important step was not:

- inserting one new object and reading one document

The important step was:

- gathering repeated mentions already accumulated in the space
- rereading them together
- producing one connected meaning sentence about how that concept exists in the space

This indicates that line must grow from:

- `document lens`

to:

- `space reread lens`

## line as document lens

Current working role:

- reads linked segments inside a document
- shows `strong / weak / absent / caution`
- keeps active / parked / candidate boundaries visible
- supports surfaced observation and operating UI

This role remains valid.

## line as space reread lens

Next directional role:

- rereads repeated meanings across the internal corpus
- gathers how a concept is spoken in:
  - reports
  - declarations
  - directives
  - transcripts
  - bridge notes
  - prior analyses
- forms one longer connected meaning sentence about:
  - what that concept currently is in the space
  - what tension or ambiguity surrounds it
  - whether it is embraced, resisted, parked, or kept as a candidate

## what this does not mean

- this does not mean line becomes a premature ontology
- this does not mean runtime should immediately auto-synthesize philosophical verdicts
- this does not mean every line must instantly become a corpus-scale reread line
- this does not mean segment-level reading is replaced

Instead, it means:

- segment-level reading remains the lower layer
- corpus-scale reread becomes an upper meaning layer

## practical implication

Future line questions should include both layers.

Lower-layer questions:

- where does this line appear in a document?
- how strong or weak is it locally?

Upper-layer questions:

- what repeated meaning does this line gather across the space?
- when rereading the internal corpus through this line, what long meaning sentence appears?
- what does the concept become in the space, not just in one source?

## why this matters for user usability

Without this shift, line remains too close to engine-native language:

- weak
- strong
- active
- parked
- thick
- thin

Those are useful internally, but they do not yet tell the user:

- what the concept means in the space
- why that meaning matters
- what tension or direction it carries

Line as a `space reread lens` is the first move toward making the space readable as thought, not only as internal reaction.

## examples already seen

### ontology

The reread did not stop at:

- active
- weak
- parked

It moved toward:

- ontology as a repeatedly desired but still dangerous ordering candidate

### harness

The reread did not stop at:

- tool structure
- latent line signal

It moved toward:

- harness as an operating form that absorbs work and establishes alignment before autonomy

## non-goals

- no immediate runtime refactor
- no automatic corpus-scale synthesis engine in this note
- no forced promotion of all current lines into reread lines
- no maturity judgment from reread alone

## current conclusion

- line should now be understood in two layers:
  - as a document reading lens
  - as a space reread lens
- the recent ontology/harness rereads are early examples of this upper-layer role
- the next serious step is not more internal labels alone, but letting line gather repeated meanings from the internal corpus into user-readable connected meaning sentences
