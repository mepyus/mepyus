# VectorFL Paper Proper Promotion Checklist v0

## purpose
This checklist fixes the moment when the validated pilot seam should be promoted into VectorFL Paper proper.

## checklist
1. actual exported host record replaces the placeholder slot occupant
2. `v4` packet is materialized from the actual export
3. runtime write-back and reopen still hold under the same seam
4. weekend naming is dropped from promoted manifests and surfaces
5. supervisor-readable reporting remains intact

## do not change during promotion
- thin issue/run/result/governance overlay
- line-centered translation categories
- write-back and reopen meaning

## promotion meaning
Promotion is not redesign.
It is naming absorption after the final source-realism gate is passed.
