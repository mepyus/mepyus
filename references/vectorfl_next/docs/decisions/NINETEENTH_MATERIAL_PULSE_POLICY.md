# Nineteenth Material Pulse Policy

## Decision

After the eighteenth pulse rethreaded observer-facing and temporal-project exposure, the nineteenth step sends one `observer-reflective` pulse inside the already exposed resonant terrain.

## Why

- The next question is no longer whether pulse behavior can widen space, but whether a pulse can reshape flow inside an already exposed component.
- This keeps observation space-first while testing whether internal flow can shift without requiring stronger bridge persistence.
- The pulse should remain a passing field, not a new dominant terrain.

## Constraints

- The pulse must form its own cell and local space before any bridge exposure appears.
- Both bridge traces must remain weak exposure only.
- The pulse must not merge observer-facing and reflective terrains.
- The step should change internal flow shape without creating a collapse.
