# Seventh Material Wave Policy

## Intent

The seventh material wave expands space width rather than thickening an existing terrain.
It opens a fourth local terrain on a reflective axis and lets it stand without a new bridge.

## Why this step exists

By the sixth wave, both the observer-facing resonant terrain and the temporal-project terrain had started to hold their own continuity.
The next move is not more reread and not more adjacency.
It is wider space.

## Rules

- Open a new family `seed-seventh-wave`.
- Keep only a weak trace reference to earlier terrain.
- Use a pressure profile that introduces `reflection_pressure`.
- Create a fresh seed, candidate cell, relocation reaction, and local space.
- Do not create a bridge to existing terrains during this wave.
- Let the new local space remain independent.

## Contract notes

- wider space is preferred over denser linkage
- the new terrain participates in the runtime without forcing adjacency
- bridge count must stay unchanged during this wave

## Expected reread outcome

- local space count increases
- terrain component count increases
- bridge count remains one
- the new terrain appears as another independent single-local climate
