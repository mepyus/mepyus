# Tenth Material Wave Policy

## Intent

The tenth material wave continues the temporal-project terrain's self-continuity.
It does not widen space and it does not add adjacency.

## Why this step exists

The reflective terrain has now had repeated self-return.
The temporal-project terrain should also be allowed another return so the newer single-local terrains do not grow unevenly.

## Rules

- Reuse the temporal-project pressure signature from the temporal-project terrain.
- Keep the family as `seed-third-wave`.
- Add a fresh material, trace, pressure profile, and reentry seed.
- Reuse the existing temporal-project cell when the pressure matches.
- Reactivate that cell with `thickening`.
- Do not create an additional bridge.
- Do not create another terrain component.

## Contract notes

- the temporal-project terrain deepens without topology change
- bridge count must stay unchanged during this wave
- repeated continuity is still material-led and append-only

## Expected reread outcome

- thickening count increases again
- bridge count remains one
- terrain component count remains three
- the temporal-project terrain keeps pace with the reflective terrain as an independent continuing field
