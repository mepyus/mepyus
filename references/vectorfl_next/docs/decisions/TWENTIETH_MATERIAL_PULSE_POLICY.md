# Twentieth Material Pulse Policy

## Decision

After repeated pulses rethreaded the main resonant span, the twentieth step sends one `observer-drift` pulse toward the outer drift-heavy terrain.

## Why

- The next question is whether the large resonant span can reach an outer terrain without forcing collapse.
- This is a non-adjacent probe relative to the recent internal rethreading steps.
- The pulse should reveal whether the outer drift remnant stays separate, weakly joins, or slightly redraws the terrain boundary.

## Constraints

- Prefer a drift-heavy local space that is still `forming` and unbridged when one exists.
- The pulse must form its own cell and local space before any bridge exposure appears.
- The pulse must not merge observer-facing and drift-heavy terrains.
- Any difference between isolated replay and lived runtime should be preserved as process evidence.
