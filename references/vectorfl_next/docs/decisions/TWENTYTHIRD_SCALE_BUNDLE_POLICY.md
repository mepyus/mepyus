# Twenty-third Scale Bundle Policy

## Decision

Continue first-scale expansion by widening the quiet side with external-world-like inputs rather than new bridge pressure.

## Bundle shape

The bundle opens four more independent local spaces:

- one web-chatgpt residue space
- one gemini-cli residue space
- one claude-code residue space
- one youtube-note space

## Why

- The runtime should begin proving that future real-world input variety can enter without being forced into immediate relation.
- This bundle increases input diversity while preserving the same physical rule: new material may form local space without instant bridge growth.
- It helps test whether the engine can host multiple future agent and media residues in the same field.

## Constraints

- No new bridge traces may be derived in this bundle.
- Each material must form its own local space.
- The bundle should widen input diversity and quiet terrain count, not relation density.
