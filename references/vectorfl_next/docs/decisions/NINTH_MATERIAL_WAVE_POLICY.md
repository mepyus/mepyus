# Ninth Material Wave Policy

## Intent

The ninth material wave continues the reflective terrain's self-continuity.
It does not widen space and it does not add adjacency.

## Why this step exists

After the eighth wave, the reflective terrain had only its first thickening round.
One more return helps confirm that the terrain can keep its own continuity before any future bridge-facing discussion.

## Rules

- Reuse the reflective pressure signature from the reflective terrain.
- Keep the family as `seed-seventh-wave`.
- Add a fresh material, trace, pressure profile, and reentry seed.
- Reuse the existing reflective cell when the pressure matches.
- Reactivate that cell with `thickening`.
- Do not create an additional bridge.
- Do not create another terrain component.

## Contract notes

- the reflective terrain deepens without topology change
- bridge count must stay unchanged during this wave
- repeated continuity is still material-led and append-only

## Expected reread outcome

- thickening count increases again
- bridge count remains one
- terrain component count remains three
- the reflective terrain becomes less like a single relocation event and more like a continuing field
