# Sixth Material Wave Policy

## Intent

The sixth material wave does not widen the terrain graph.
It returns to the already opened observer-facing neighboring terrain and lets that side of the resonant pair thicken under the same pressure it already carries.

## Why this step exists

After the fourth and fifth waves, the temporal-project third terrain had started to hold its own repeated thickening.
The earlier resonant terrain still had one side carrying most of the held continuity.
This wave lets the neighboring observer-facing local terrain accumulate its own repeated thickening without forcing a new bridge or a new local space.

## Rules

- Reuse the tone-heavy pressure signature that opened the second-wave neighboring terrain.
- Keep the family as `seed-fresh-note`.
- Add a fresh material, trace, pressure profile, and reentry seed.
- Reuse the existing neighboring cell when the pressure matches.
- Reactivate that cell with `thickening`.
- Do not create an additional bridge.
- Do not create another terrain component.

## Contract notes

- terrain growth still happens through material reentry
- the resonant terrain gains internal maturity without collapsing
- bridge count must stay unchanged during this wave

## Expected reread outcome

- the resonant two-local terrain remains a single terrain component
- thickening count increases
- bridge count remains one
- the third terrain remains independent
