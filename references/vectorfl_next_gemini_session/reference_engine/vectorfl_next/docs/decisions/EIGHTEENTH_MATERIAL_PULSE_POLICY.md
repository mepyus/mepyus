# Eighteenth Material Pulse Policy

## Decision

After the seventeenth pulse generalized weak exposure between heterogeneous and drift-heavy terrains, the eighteenth step sends one `observer-temporal` pulse between an observer-facing terrain and the temporal-project terrain.

## Why

- The runtime now has enough width to test whether pulse-led flow changes keep generalizing across different mature terrain pairs.
- This step observes whether one passing terrain can shift component flow without merging or collapsing the spaces it touches.
- The pulse should remain smaller than the terrains it approaches.

## Constraints

- The pulse must form its own cell and local space before any bridge exposure appears.
- Both resulting bridge traces must remain weak exposure only.
- No existing terrain may be merged or rewritten.
- The step should change flow shape, not convert relation into the new center.
