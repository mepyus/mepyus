# Twelfth Material Wave Policy

## Intent

The twelfth material wave gives the heterogeneous component band its first self-return.

## Why this step exists

The eleventh wave proved that a different component band can open as an independent terrain.
This wave checks whether that terrain can also hold continuity without immediately collapsing into relation.

## Rules

- Reuse the same heterogeneous pressure band from the eleventh terrain.
- Keep the family as `seed-eleventh-wave`.
- Add a fresh material, trace, pressure profile, and reentry seed.
- Reuse the existing heterogeneous cell when the pressure matches.
- Reactivate that cell with `thickening`.
- Do not create an additional bridge.
- Do not create another terrain component.

## Contract notes

- heterogeneous terrain should get a chance to mature before relation is tested
- bridge count must stay unchanged during this wave
- continuity remains material-led and append-only

## Expected reread outcome

- thickening count increases again
- bridge count remains one
- terrain component count remains four
- the heterogeneous component band becomes a continuing field rather than a one-off relocation
