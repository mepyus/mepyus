# Twenty-second Scale Bundle Policy

## Decision

Continue first-scale expansion by widening quiet terrain again without adding new bridge pressure.

## Bundle shape

The bundle opens four more independent local spaces:

- one agent-log space
- one failed-experiment space
- one book-note space
- one unknown-fragment space

## Why

- The first scale bundle proved that quiet spaces can be added without bridge growth.
- The next question is whether the same physical rule still holds when more weakly-legible material enters the field.
- This step grows the non-relation side of the runtime so later sparse-presence review will have enough scale to matter.

## Constraints

- No new bridge traces may be derived in this bundle.
- Each material must form its own local space.
- The bundle should increase forming or quiet single-local terrain count, not relation density.
