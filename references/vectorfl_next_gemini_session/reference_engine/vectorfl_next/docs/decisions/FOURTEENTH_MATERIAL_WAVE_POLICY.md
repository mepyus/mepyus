# Fourteenth Material Wave Policy

## Intent

The fourteenth material wave gives the drift-heavy component band its first self-return.

## Why this step exists

The thirteenth wave widens the runtime with another independent component band.
This wave checks whether that band can also hold continuity before any relation pressure is introduced.

## Rules

- Reuse the same latency-drift-ambiguity pressure band from the thirteenth terrain.
- Keep the family as `seed-thirteenth-wave`.
- Add a fresh material, trace, pressure profile, and reentry seed.
- Reuse the existing drift-heavy cell when the pressure matches.
- Reactivate that cell with `thickening`.
- Do not create an additional bridge.
- Do not create another terrain component.

## Contract notes

- new heterogeneous terrain should get a chance to mature before relation is tested
- bridge count must stay unchanged during this wave
- continuity remains material-led and append-only

## Expected reread outcome

- thickening count increases again
- bridge count remains one
- terrain component count remains five
- the drift-heavy component band becomes a continuing field rather than a one-off relocation
