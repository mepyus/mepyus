# Fifteenth Bridge Exposure Policy

## Intent

The fifteenth step does not create a new terrain.
It tests whether a weak bridge-facing exposure can emerge between two already mature independent terrains.

## Why this step exists

The runtime has already widened into multiple independent terrains.
Before stronger relation is considered, it is time to see whether a weak exposure can appear without collapsing those terrains.

## Rules

- Do not create a new material family.
- Use the already matured `seed-third-wave` temporal-project terrain.
- Use the already matured `seed-seventh-wave` reflective terrain.
- Derive a bridge trace from the two local spaces.
- Accept only `observed` or `candidate` style exposure in this step.
- Do not merge or collapse the two terrains.

## Contract notes

- relation is tested only after sufficient width and self-return
- bridge remains an exposure, not a merge trigger
- the two terrains must remain distinct local spaces after the exposure

## Expected reread outcome

- bridge count increases by one
- terrain component count decreases by one because two independent terrains now sit in one exposed component
- the bridge should read as weak exposure rather than durable holding
