# Sparse Presence Review Policy

## Decision

Sparse-presence review is now allowed as an ongoing descriptive check during scale growth.

## Important limit

This is **not** the final sparse-presence verdict.

It is only a running review that asks:

- how many quiet local spaces currently exist
- how many remain unbridged or weakly legible
- whether quiet terrain is continuing to widen alongside resonant terrain

## Rule

- review may guide observation
- review may not collapse quiet space into an early pass/fail judgment
- sparse-presence review remains descriptive until the larger parked checkpoint is revisited later
