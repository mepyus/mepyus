# Twenty-Sixth Scale Bundle Policy

## Purpose

The twenty-sixth scale bundle widens the quiet side again without increasing bridge pressure. It tests whether the same first-scale physics still hold when additional weakly legible materials enter as independent local spaces rather than immediate relations.

## What enters

- handoff residue
- tool error residue
- unread quote residue
- question residue

## Contract

- no new bridge is required for this bundle
- each new material may form its own local space if pressure does not justify immediate relation
- quiet, weakly legible, and not-yet-purposeful material remains valid space
- append-only history remains intact

## Why this matters

The engine should not only host resonant and visible formations. It must also host quiet operational residue, unresolved conceptual material, and reading fragments that can remain present without immediate extraction.
